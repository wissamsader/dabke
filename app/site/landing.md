# DABKE

DABKE is an Arabic wedding keyboard for the M-VAVE FM-1, made by STUDIOMORPHIK. It plays maqam scales with quarter
tones on any note over dabke and shaabi rhythms, with mijwiz, zurna, oud, qanun and nine more voices, a drone,
trills and glides. Lean on a key and the note bends. Eight tracks with loops and a step sequencer, and an Akai MIDImix
works as its mixer.

![DABKE's home screen: the singer on his stage](manual/img/screen-home.png "DABKE on the FM-1's screen")

This is version {{VERSION}}, a public beta. You install it at your own risk, and the installer can put M-VAVE's own
firmware back.

- [Play it in the browser](emu/). It runs the same code as the FM-1, so you don't need one.
- [Install](install/) it from Chrome or Edge, with the FM-1 connected by USB.
- The [Manual](manual/) explains the pages, the tracks, recording, saving and the MIDImix.
- [Download {{PKG}}](firmware/{{PKG}}) for any FM-1 installer that takes a `.fwsc` package.
- The [Source](https://github.com/wissamsader/dabke) is under MPL-2.0. It's built on fm1-base and felucca-bsp, from
  Felucca by Leo Kuroshita, Hügelton Instruments.
