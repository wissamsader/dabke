# HOLD (EDIT tapped on any page, SOLO on the MIDImix), as he plays it. From the repository's root:
#   app/build/host/dabke_host app/tests/hold.txt OUTDIR
# Pressed with keys down, their notes sound on after the keys are let go; with other keys down, those are held
# instead; with none down, the held notes go. Played meanwhile, a key is not held. 0.19: the key of a held note,
# pressed again, lets that note go at once and plays nothing (whatever the octave or the track picked now); OCT- and
# OCT+ move the held notes with their keys (the left keys as set stay, as their keys do), and they stay held; the
# track's PITCH, TRANSPOSE and a quarter tone move them too; a track's new sound takes its held notes over; nothing
# stays stuck. Up to 0.18 a held key pressed again struck its note anew and let it go only when lifted, after OCT- or
# OCT+ the held notes stayed where they were and their keys could no longer let them go, a held key pressed with
# another track picked darkened its light and left its note held, and a new sound on a track let its held notes go.
# (toneC: the output's level at the pitch C cents above MIDI note 0, 6000 middle C, dB of full scale, measured on the
# last 186 ms: a held note of the ORGAN reads about -30, one gone below -60; an octave above a note sounding reads its
# harmonic, so a note moved up is checked by its old pitch gone. Key 7 is C4, 9 D4, 11 E (a quarter flat in BAYATI),
# 14 G4, 19 C5)
wait 900
expect track 0
