# The keys' pressure belongs to the track under the fingers. From the repository's root:
#   app/build/host/dabke_host app/tests/pressure.txt OUTDIR
# A key leant on bends, shakes or brightens the notes its own track plays from the keys, by
# PRESSURE (the ornaments page, row 2, K4), whatever note sound is on that track: the lead, the left hand's sound, the
# bass line. Up to 0.12 the pressure was read only from keys playing track 1, and only the lead's family of sounds
# bent: with another track picked a lean did nothing (his report, 11-15: "i put bend down and it doesn't bend down").
# A drum track's keys have no pressure. (key K down VEL LEAN: the lean in %; pitch_x10: what sounds, tenths of a hertz)
wait 900
expect track 0
expect ch11 1
expect ch12 1
# (the lead's automatic ornaments off, so a held note's pitch is the note's: the ornaments page, row 1, K4)
tap LFO
turn SELECT -20
wait 30
turn K4 -20
wait 30
expect ch3 0
tap HOME
wait 30

# ---- ALL picked: the lead, BEND UP as it starts. Leant on: up; eased off: back

wait 900
expect pressure >100
expect pitch_x10 >3620
expect pitch_x10 <3720
key 12 down 100 0
wait 900
expect pressure 0
expect pitch_x10 >3456
expect pitch_x10 <3516
key 12 up
